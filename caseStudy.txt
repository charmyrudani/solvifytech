Wines of NZ operates a multi-vendor marketplace where multiple suppliers manage products and inventory.
Traditional multi-vendor Shopify setups created operational challenges:
Vendors needed product management access.
Product information became inconsistent.
Pricing changes happened without oversight.
Inventory was not always accurate.
